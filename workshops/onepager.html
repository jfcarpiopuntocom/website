The problem. Two costs sit in most teams and never show up as a line item: how people handle their own money, and how they read the company that pays them. Both leak out of focus, initiative, and goodwill, every pay period.

Two independent, modular workshops

Workshop 1 · 2 hours

Money & Personal/Family Finance

From financial chaos and anxiety to control. Live exercises on each person's real numbers: liquidity, debt, savings, investment.

Outcome: two or three dated money decisions per participant.

Workshop 2 · 2 hours

Appreciation of Private Enterprise

From win-lose to win-win. Each person builds their own employment P&L and the resentment narrative is replaced with arithmetic.

Outcome: ownership, initiative, a cooler read of salary, profit, and margin.

Why two hours sticks: the sessions run on MindOS (boundary → behavior → intellect → decision). Most training enters at the intellect, on beliefs no one examined, so it fades in two weeks. Changing the order is what makes the new behavior dated and observable.
