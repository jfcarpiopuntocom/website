Stock Semaphore

Support

For help with Stock Semaphore, include the prompt you tried, the inventory fields you supplied, and what you expected to see. Do not send passwords, PINs, license keys, payment information, or customer records.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm each item has at least a product name and stock quantity.
  • For margin classification, include price and cost.
  • For dead-stock classification, include days since last sale.
  • For perishable warnings, include days until expiry.

Use the JFCarpio.com contact page for support requests.
