Acquisition FAQ

What exactly is for sale?

The complete La Escuela del Dinero brand: 12 digital financial-education products (video courses, a fully-voiced audiobook, ebooks and assessments), the full 12-week curriculum and content library, the domain and Gumroad storefront, the customer and email list, all social accounts (TikTok, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) and the Telegram community. A 100% turnkey transfer of brand, IP and audience.

Who is this acquisition opportunity for?

Capital partners and operators who want to own a category in the 500-million-person Spanish-speaking financial-education market: family offices and private investors seeking Latin American and U.S. Hispanic exposure, and agile education, media or fintech companies expanding into Spanish-language content.

What market does La Escuela del Dinero serve?

Spanish-speaking consumers across the U.S. Hispanic population, Latin America and Spain: over 500 million native speakers, in a category with documented financial-literacy demand and no dominant branded competitor.

Is the business validated?

Yes. It has real paying customers acquired organically with effectively zero advertising, multiple products rated a perfect 5.0, and a built free-to-paid funnel. The two largest growth levers, a marketing budget and a brand spokesperson, have never been activated.
