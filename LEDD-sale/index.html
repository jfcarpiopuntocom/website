The whitespace

A massive audience, no incumbent to displace.

Spanish-language financial education is dominated by hotel-seminar sellers and re-packagers of a handful of U.S. finance books. There is no trusted, scalable, productized brand serving 500 million people. The first credible player defines the category.

The asset

Not a concept. A built business.

  • 12 digital products across video, audio, ebooks and assessments.
  • A flagship 12-week program with an integrated community model.
  • Real paying customers and multiple products rated a perfect 5.0.
  • Established brand, domain, storefront and PR foundation.
The edge

The product works. It was never given a budget or a face.

Every customer to date was earned with effectively zero paid advertising and no public spokesperson. The two highest-impact growth levers are still at zero. Add capital and distribution to a proven product, and you scale into category leadership.
