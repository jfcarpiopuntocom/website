A tale of two businesses · 05

This is not a story of heroes and fools.

Both businesses may work equally hard. The accumulated difference is less dramatic and more useful: one reduces blind spots; the other keeps more places where a problem can grow unseen.

Business A Tools + workshops + reports + dashboards reduce blind spots and connect information with action.

Business B Relies more on memory, late reaction and decisions rebuilt from scratch.

B 17 In the end, the second business carries:

  1. memory and late counts
  2. stock and sales that don't match
  3. finds out after the fact
  4. everyone improvises
  5. accepts stories that blame it all on him
  6. does not understand why its costs keep rising
  7. copies recipes without checking where they come from
  8. reacts once the consequence has arrived
  9. decides with old maps
  10. rebuilds the data every time
  11. does not know its margin or how much debt it carries
  12. only checks its stability when cash gets tight
  13. loses focus, profit and cooperation
  14. pays dearly for what was already solved
  15. mixes household money with business money
  16. does not even take what was free
  17. has no one to ask who has seen it before
