Two kinds of business builders · 05

There are not two kinds of businesses. There are two ways to run them.

All of us can operate both ways. The difference is whether we wait until the problem becomes obvious or build better ways to see it while there is still room to act.

Sees earlier Tools + workshops + reports + dashboards reduce blind spots and connect information with action.

Finds out later Relies more on memory, late reaction and decisions rebuilt from scratch.
