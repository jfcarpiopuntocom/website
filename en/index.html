Who is behind the work

The JFCarpio.com team

It is led by Juan Fernando Carpio, an economic and business researcher who has spent 25 years studying why businesses fail and how to avoid it. He taught economics and business for 14 years, founded the first Austrian Economics and Institutional sub-department in Ecuador, and collaborated in the creation of the USFQ Economics Institute and its journal, Polemika.

Today, the JFCarpio.com team, with external specialists and proprietary tools, turns that research into software, reports, and consulting, in Spanish and English.
