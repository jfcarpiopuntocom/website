What we review

Ready to show. Ready to use?

A launch checklist organized by what the person who lands on your product will see and experience.

Get found

Search and social

What decides whether people reach your product.

  • Search title and description
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml
  • Share image (Open Graph)
  • Favicon and alt text
Make it work

Real-world experience

What happens when someone actually uses it.

  • Responsive mobile design
  • Custom 404 page
  • Clear loading states
  • Errors that guide
  • WebP images
Build trust

Signals of credibility

What makes someone willing to pay or leave their details.

  • Terms and conditions
  • Cookies, if applicable
  • Analytics with a purpose
  • Real contact options
From problem to deliverable

A review with clear limits and results.

You receive the issues found and a priority order for fixing them.

01 · The problem

Share the URL and the journey that worries you.

Plus who it serves and the planned launch date. No passwords or private data.

02 · The process

We agree on pages, devices and criteria.

Scope, timeline and price are set before we start.

03 · The deliverable

A list of findings with priorities.

With location and user impact. Your team decides what to fix first.

Examples of what may come up: a button that fails on mobile, an error that does not explain how to recover, a pricing page that does not say what you get.

What is not included by default

A review, not a certification.

This is not a penetration audit, a sales guarantee or a promise to fix every defect. Any later implementation is agreed separately.
