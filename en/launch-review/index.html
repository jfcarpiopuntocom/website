READY TO SHOW. READY TO USE?

The job does not end when the main screen looks good. A missing page, an unhelpful error or a broken mobile layout may be the first thing someone sees when deciding whether to trust your product.

You receive the problems found and an order of priorities for fixing them. Before work begins, we agree on the pages and user journeys to review, the delivery date and the price.

WHAT WE REVIEW

A launch checklist organized around what people will see and experience when they reach your product.

01 / DISCOVER

BE FINDABLE

  • Meta title on every page
  • Useful meta description
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml
  • Open Graph image for sharing
  • Recognizable favicon
  • Alt text for images
02 / USE

WORK IN PRACTICE

  • Responsive mobile layout
  • Custom 404 page
  • Clear loading states
  • Error messages that help people recover
  • Compressed WebP images
03 / TRUST

EARN TRUST

  • Terms and conditions
  • Cookies, where applicable
  • Purposeful analytics
  • Real ways to contact you

FROM THE PROBLEM TO THE DELIVERABLE

A REVIEW WITH A CLEAR SCOPE AND OUTPUT.

  1. 01 · The problem. Share your URL, intended user, launch date and the journey that concerns you most. No passwords or private user data.
  2. 02 · The process. We agree on pages, devices, scenarios and review criteria. Scope, schedule and price are agreed before work begins.
  3. 03 · The deliverable. You get specific findings with their location, customer impact and repair priority. Your team can decide what to fix first.

EXAMPLES OF WHAT WE MAY FIND

A button that fails on mobile. An error that offers no recovery path. A pricing page that looks finished but never explains what customers receive. These are review examples, not client case studies or promised outcomes.

WHAT IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY INCLUDED

This is not a certification, a penetration test, a sales guarantee or a promise to fix every defect. Implementation afterward, if needed, is scoped separately.
