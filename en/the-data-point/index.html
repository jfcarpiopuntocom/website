Surviving is not scaling: reading late still costs cash, investment and team.

In Ecuador, INEC showed that of every 100 businesses born in 2015 only 67 were still active the following year. GEM Ecuador 2024 adds the recent context: blackouts, insecurity, unstable regulation and limited credit.

The lesson is not dramatic; it is practical. Wealth is protected before the blow is obvious: by reading currency, cash, incentives, regulation and environmental signals early enough.
