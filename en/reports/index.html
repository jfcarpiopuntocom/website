How it works

  1. Send your question

    Tell us what you need to decide, your business context and deadline. We confirm that the scope fits a focused report before you commit.

  2. Complete a short questionnaire

    We send the questions needed to understand the situation. Include relevant figures or documents; do not send unnecessary personal or customer data.

  3. Have a 10-minute online interview

    We clarify the question, assumptions and available evidence. English and Spanish are available.

  4. Receive your report within 72 hours

    You receive findings, important uncertainties, options and practical next steps in writing. The delivery clock begins after the interview and receipt of the complete agreed inputs.
