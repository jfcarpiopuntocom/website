Consignment statistics · United States

What US consignment shops pay their consignors.

We read the published consignor agreements of 20 independent shops, from Dallas to Buffalo and from Santa Monica to Cambridge. Clothing, kids' gear, furniture, crafts and building salvage. Here is what they pay, for how long they hold an item, and when the money goes out.

Data reviewed November 10, 2026. Every number links to its source below.

The JFCarpio.com Consignor Split 50%

Median share of the sale price that a US consignment shop pays the consignor on a $60 item, across 18 published agreements.

Average: 48.3%. Six shops pay 40%, eight pay 50%, and only three pay more than half.

Six findings

Half is the norm. The rules around it are not.

8 of 18

Half is the most common split

Eight shops pay 50% on a $60 item and six pay 40%. Only Thrift Pony and Craftworks (60%) and Rhealana (65%) pay more than half. The average is 48.3%.

10 of 20

Many shops run several splits at once

Half of the shops change the consignor's share by price, category, time on the floor or consignor status. Clotheshorse Anonymous pays 25% on items of $25 and under and 80% on sales of $5,000 or more. Modern Resale pays 50% in the first three months, 40% up to month nine and 30% after that.

90 days

The usual clock

Of the 17 shops that state a term, seven use exactly 90 days, and the median is 90. Terms run from 60 days to a full year.

Day 30

When the first markdown lands

At least seven shops publish a markdown schedule tied to days on the floor, and six of them make the first cut at day 30. Wiggle Worms takes 25% off after 30 days, 50% after 60 and 75% after 90.

4 of 20

Store credit pays more

At least four shops pay more when the consignor takes store credit instead of cash. What's New Furniture pays 50% in cash or 60% in store credit; Great Labels raises 40% to 50% when proceeds are spent in the store.

No standard

Payday varies shop by shop

Clotheshorse Anonymous pays on the 1st of each month and Furniture Consignment Group on the 20th of the following month. Jack & Juni pays once a balance reaches $100. Modern Resale pays 15 to 30 days after the buyer takes delivery.

For shop owners

One check, four rules.

Put these terms together and a single consignor check depends on the item's price tier, the day it sold, the markdown in force that day and how the consignor wants to be paid. Then repeat it for every consignor, every month.

Method

How we counted.

  • We read each shop's own consignor page or signed-agreement template, as published online on November 10, 2026.
  • The JFCarpio.com Consignor Split is the median consignor share on a $60 item, the cash rate at the start of the term. Eighteen of the 20 shops publish a rate that applies to that item.
  • "Use more than one split" counts shops whose rate changes with price, category, time on the floor or consignor status. Markdown, store-credit and payday counts are minimums: some shops do not publish those terms.
  • We left out shops outside the US, agreements dated before 2020, pages with no location, and online platforms without a fixed rate.
  • This is a reviewed sample, not a random survey. Terms change, so check the source before relying on any single shop's numbers.
