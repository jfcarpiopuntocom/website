Questions worth answering before you start.

Is the sample store my 30-day trial?

No. PIN 456 opens a prepared example store. PIN 789 activates your own store and starts its full 30-day trial.

Do I have to assign every sale to a team member?

No. Counter Sale can record a sale for the house, without assigning an associate or commission.

What if I work without internet?

The app is local-first. You can keep working on your device offline. Sharing changes with other devices requires reconnection.

Can I keep a copy of my data?

Yes. The app includes an export you control. Make and safely keep your own backup regularly—especially before changing devices.

What if I lose my PIN or need help?

Contact J. F. Carpio on WhatsApp. Please do not send your license or private business information in a public message.
