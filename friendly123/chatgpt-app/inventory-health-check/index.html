Inventory Health Check

Paste inventory. See what needs attention first.

Previously developed under the working name Stock Semaphore.

Inventory Health Check is a small ChatGPT utility for shop owners and operators. It classifies each item as Urgent, Low, Dead weight, Star, or Healthy using stock levels, days since last sale, gross margin, and optional expiry information.

What it does

  • Flags out-of-stock and low-stock items.
  • Surfaces items that have not sold for 45+ days.
  • Highlights strong-margin items when no more severe signal applies.
  • Raises the alert for perishable items approaching expiry.
  • Sorts the result by attention level and shows it as a compact color-coded card inside ChatGPT.

What it does not do

It is not a POS, accounting system, food-safety system, purchasing agent, or automatic ordering tool. It does not change your inventory or send orders.

Privacy by design

The Inventory Health Check MCP tool is stateless. It computes the classification and returns it. It does not maintain an inventory database or forward inventory to another service. Information pasted into ChatGPT is still handled by OpenAI under the policies and settings that apply to your ChatGPT use.
